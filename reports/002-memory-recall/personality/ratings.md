Two samples per condition, one rater, one opening line. The numbers
can show a difference this large or larger; they cannot measure one.
H4 asked for at least one preset a point or more warmer than the
control; the largest gap was +1.00.

Niko's own note after rating: all twelve were friendly and met social
etiquette. For the preference score, being addressed by name is the
warmest thing a reply can do; he is in his 40s and is not doing
emoji talk; and the shorter the better for long-term cognitive load.
